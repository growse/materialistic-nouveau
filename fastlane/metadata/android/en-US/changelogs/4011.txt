A new Classic theme brings back the original Materialistic orange, and Sepia and Solarized are orange again. On Android 15 and later the status bar is now solid, so the toolbar and stories no longer slide behind the clock. Voting while signed out now offers to log in, a story list that fails to load shows an error instead of staying blank, story cards no longer all look selected, and responses are cached as intended for offline reading. Also includes dependency updates.
